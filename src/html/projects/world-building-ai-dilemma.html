World Building: The AI Dilemma

A comparative experiment on how AI changes creative world building in sci-fi writing

Overview and Summary

"World Building: The AI Dilemma" is a structured creative and technical experiment that investigates the role of AI in the creative writing process, specifically within sci-fi world building.


The project constructs three distinct fictional worlds using separate methodologies: one developed entirely by a human author, one generated independently by AI, and one produced through an iterative human-AI collaborative workflow.

Project Information

Timeline

March 2026 - Present

Development Method

Comparative research across human-only, AI-only, and human-AI collaborative workflows

Primary Tech Stack

Generative AI tools, structured creative writing, and research analysis frameworks

Focus

Evaluating AI impact on originality, consistency, and creative voice in sci-fi world building

Purpose and Research Questions

  • Does AI enhance or hinder creative world building?
  • What strengths does each method bring to originality, depth, and consistency?
  • Is AI collaboration worth it, or does it dilute the writer's voice?

Three-World Framework

World 1 - Human Only

Built entirely without AI assistance to evaluate originality, personal voice, and unassisted creative depth.

World 2 - AI Only

Generated independently by AI to evaluate speed, breadth, and internal consistency of machine-created world building.

World 3 - Human + AI Collaboration

Developed through iterative dialogue between author and AI to evaluate synergy, creative expansion, and co-authorship dynamics.

Comparison Criteria

  • Originality and creative voice
  • Depth and internal consistency
  • Efficiency and ease of process
  • Emotional resonance and authenticity
  • Limitations and tradeoffs of each method

Technologies and Skills

AI-Assisted Ideation

Prompting strategies, iterative refinement, and comparative output analysis

Creative Writing

Narrative world design, lore construction, and voice control across methods

Research Design

Controlled methodology, criteria-based comparison, and reflection-driven conclusions

Critical Analysis

Tradeoff mapping for originality, speed, consistency, and creative ownership

Final Deliverables

  • Three Complete Worlds: One from each methodology (human-only, AI-only, collaborative)
  • Comparative Analysis Paper: Structured findings based on the defined framework
  • Reflective Conclusion: A practical perspective on how AI should be used in creative writing
